Independently certified.

Prapthi.ai is operated by Legal Care AI Private Limited, whose management systems have been audited by external certification bodies against three international standards. Each certificate is published below in full, so you can read the scope and check it with the body that issued it.

The certificates

Three standards, externally audited.

ISO/IEC 27001:2022

Information security

The standard for an information security management system — how information is classified, who may reach it, how risks are assessed and how incidents are handled.

Certified scope

All three certificates are issued to Legal Care AI Private Limited, Sy No. 86, 87, 88, 505/B-508, Sandhya Techno-1, Raidurg, Hyderabad, Rangareddy, Telangana 500104, India — the same company named in our Terms, Privacy Policy and Refund Policy.

The certified scope is the provision of artificial intelligence (AI) powered legal technology platforms, custom software development, digital information service platforms, contract lifecycle automation, enterprise IT management, and strategic management consultancy services. The ISO/IEC 42001 certificate also lists information technology services. The exact wording for each standard is printed on its own certificate.

What this does and does not mean

These certificates cover the company’s management systems — the way we run information security, AI governance and quality — and not any individual product. As Robust Certifications states on the ISO 9001 certificate, the registration is not a product quality certificate.

Each certificate stays valid only while surveillance audits are completed on schedule. The first surveillance audits are due in September 2027 and the second in September 2028.

Verifying these certificates

Do not take our word for it. ISO/IEC 27001 and ISO/IEC 42001 were issued by Veritas Management System UK Ltd, 33 Summit House, Hanworth Road, Sunbury-on-Thames TW16 5DA, London, United Kingdom, which publishes its client list at iso-uk.co.uk. ISO 9001 was issued by Robust Certifications Pvt. Ltd., H-15, Sector-63, Noida, Gautam Buddha Nagar 201304, Uttar Pradesh, which verifies certificates at robustcertifications.com.

If anything here does not match what a certification body tells you, please tell us — we would rather fix the page than have you trust it.
