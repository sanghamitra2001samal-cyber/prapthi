01

Natural rhythm

Good speech combines changes in pitch, duration and stress. Listening to an entire phrase is more informative than judging isolated words.

02

Multilingual clarity

Pronunciation, local names, number formats and switching between languages all affect how understandable speech feels.

03

Human evaluation

Compare the same passages with consistent listening conditions. Ask listeners about clarity and expression separately, and include people who speak each target language.
