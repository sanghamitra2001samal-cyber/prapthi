01

Write for the ear

Use short sentences, clear punctuation and familiar language. Read the text aloud once before generating a preview.

02

Compare one change at a time

Keep the text fixed while you change the emotion or speaking style. This makes differences in pace and emphasis easier to judge.

03

Working with multilingual text

Select the intended language, check unfamiliar names carefully and listen to the complete passage. Browser voice support depends on your device.
