# Prapthi voices and languages

Taken from the live server on 6 Nov 2026, after all voices were switched on.

- **280 voices** in **56 languages**: 19 Indian languages (156 voices) and 37 others (124 voices).
- 177 are Premium (paid plans only) and 103 are Free.
- 93 are **slow** (Parler HD): about 30 seconds for a short sentence, because they run on the website server's processor, not the GPU. They were hidden from customers until 6 Nov 2026.
- Not counted here: each customer's own cloned voices.
